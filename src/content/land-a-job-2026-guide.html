Land a job in 2026

Tech market is not dead. Generic applications are. Public job posts can get flooded, so the priority is getting to a real person before your CV disappears into a pile.

My simple system: reach a human, tailor the CV, negotiate properly.

01

Find the human

CREA ready agent: Cold DM

Scan companies that are actually hiring, then find someone inside: an employee, hiring manager or recruiter. Referrals and direct intros beat blindly sending the same application everywhere.

Calibrate with: target role · location · company type · your background · your tone
02

Make the CV match

CREA ready agent: CV Optimizer

Do not send one generic CV to every role. Give the agent the job description plus your real experience, then let it tune the wording for the role without inventing achievements.

Calibrate with: job description · master CV · must-keep wins · keywords
03

Negotiate once they want you

CREA ready agent: Salary Negotiation Coach

When the offer comes, rehearse the conversation. Give the agent the offer, your target number and your leverage, then make it push back until your answer sounds natural.

Calibrate with: current offer · target salary · market range · leverage · walk-away point

CREA already simplifies the automation. The useful part is calibrating each agent to your own requirements.
