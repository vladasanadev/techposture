01

What you will make

One talking-head take, about 35 seconds. You speak editing commands. Claude turns every command into a visible edit at the moment you say it. Nothing in it is AI-generated: every pixel is your own footage, code-drawn graphics and synthesised sound.

You sayWhat happens
“ChatGPT or Claude?”Hook title from frame 0, name tags pop onto your palms, VS on “or”
“…in real time”Your video shrinks into a live editor with two AI cursors and a LIVE timecode
“Put ChatGPT logo on my right hand”The cursor clicks a glass 3D cube onto your palm, and it follows your hand
“Make them float and fly off”The cubes hover, sway, then fling off to the sides
“Let's make it harder”Snap punch-in, shake, difficulty meter
“Put subtitles…”Two-font captions start on that word
“…any Tarantino movie”A frameless 3D film card slides out from behind your head
“Clear everything on the background”A light-band wipe swaps your room for a clean sky
“…a clone of me next to me”A copy of you slides out into a two-shot
“So who won?”The cubes come back to frame the question
“Want to edit videos like this too?”Your real footage and captions. Clean ending.

02

What you need

01

Claude Code

The terminal app or the Code tab in the Claude desktop app, on a paid Claude plan.

02

A good machine

An Apple Silicon Mac is best. A PC with an NVIDIA GPU works too. About 6 GB free.

03

Three free tools

ffmpeg, Google Chrome and Python 3.12. Claude installs the rest.

04

Your take

One landscape video, 30 fps, about 35 seconds. A movie clip too, if you want that beat.

05

~5 min setup

Once. Claude installs the engine and downloads the models.

06

~20 min per edit

Mostly rendering. You check the plan and the result.

Why it runs on your computer. The clone, the wipe and the film card behind your hair need a proper cut-out of you on every frame. That's real compute. A laptop does it in about a minute, no credits needed.

03

The script and how to film it

Read this on camera. Swap the AIs (Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Meta AI, Le Chat, DeepSeek and Copilot work too) or the movie if you want. Skip a line and that effect is skipped.

The script
Who's better at video editing, ChatGPT or Claude?
Let's make them edit this video in real time.
Put ChatGPT logo on my right hand.
Now put Claude logo on my left hand.
Now make them float and fly off to the sides.
Let's make it harder.
Put subtitles using these two fonts.
Overlay a clip from any Tarantino movie when I say "movie".
Let's try even harder.
Clear everything on the background and put a clone of me next to me.
So... who won?
Want to edit videos like this too? Comment EDIT and I'll send you my instructions.
Landscape 16:94K or 1080p, 30 fps, phone on a tripod at eye level. The 9:16 version comes from the head-to-head step.
Mid-chest up, centredLeave room on both sides for your raised palms.
Palms up and openWhen you name a hand, raise it beside your shoulder and keep both up until “fly off”. No palm, no cube.
Any backgroundA plain wall is easiest. A bright window works, there's a cleanup setting for it.
Jump cuts are fineGlue your best takes together. Claude finds the cuts and uses them.

05

The prompts

1 · Setup, once
Use the reel-live-edit skill. Run its setup and tell me when it prints READY.
If anything is missing (ffmpeg, Chrome, Python 3.12), tell me the exact command to install it.
2 · The edit
Use the reel-live-edit skill to edit my take: <path/to/take.mov>
The two AIs are ChatGPT (my right hand) and Claude (my left hand).
For the "movie" command use <path/to/clip.mp4>, starting at <m:ss> in that file.
Work in <~/live-edit/my-take>.
Before the long steps, show me the plan: every command, the word it lands on and the time.
Then render it, open the QA sheet, check every beat yourself, and give me the MP4 path.
No music, no AI-generated footage. Keep the ending clean (my footage + captions only).
Ask for one change at a time after the first render. Claude tests each change on stills before re-rendering, so small fixes take a minute, not ten.

06

Check the plan, then the result

Before anything heavy runs, Claude shows you a table: every beat, its time, and the words around it. Read it once. “chat gbt” is just how the transcriber spells ChatGPT. Your right hand shows on the left of the screen.

What the plan looks like
  7.18  logos.left        …chat gbt logo on my…
 10.28  logos.right       …put claude logo on my…
 12.70  logos.float       …make them float and fly…
 15.10  difficulty HARDER …the sides let's make it…
 20.17  clip.in           …from any tarantino movie when…
 26.40  clone.t           …put a clone of me…

After the render, Claude makes a QA sheet with one frame just after every beat, checks it, and gives you the file. Then you watch it.

Fixes that come up
The ChatGPT cube appears a bit late. Move its click 3 frames earlier, show me a still, then re-render.
Make the movie card bigger (about 1000 px wide) and start the clip on the moment he raises his hands.
Captions: split "AND I'LL SEND YOU" into "AND I'LL" / "send you".

07

The head-to-head reel

Give another AI the exact same take and the exact same brief, then stack both edits into the 9:16 “who edited it better” reel. Use your original audio so neither edit's sound effects tip it.

The fair brief (give it to every AI)
Here is my talking-head take: <file>. I speak editing commands in it. Edit the video so every command visibly happens on the word I say it: logos on the hands I name, they float and fly off, it gets "harder", subtitles in two fonts start when I say "subtitles", a clip from a Tarantino movie appears when I say "Tarantino", the background clears, a clone of me appears next to me, and the ending is clean with captions. Every second should look edited. Deliver a 1920×1080 MP4, same length, in sync with my audio.
Stack them
Use the reel-live-edit skill to stack two edits of the same take into the 9:16 head-to-head:
top = <claude_edit.mp4> labelled "CLAUDE" (brand claude),
bottom = <other_edit.mp4> labelled "CHATGPT" (brand chatgpt).
Title "EDITING" until the end of my first sentence, then "WHO'S better?". Use my original take's audio.
Show me a frame before you send the file.

08

Quick fixes

A cube floats in the airYour palm wasn't up yet. Ask Claude to check the palm tracking at that time, or delay the click.
Cubes on the wrong hands“Swap the brands.” If your camera mirrors you, say “my take is mirrored”.
Room left in your hair after the wipe“Turn on the window matte cleanup.” Holes in dark hair instead? Turn it off.
The film card covers your face“Put the clip on the other side” or “make it smaller”.
A beat on the wrong word“Move X to the word Y.” Claude edits the plan and checks a still.

Use movie clips you have the right to use. Brand logos are there to name the products you compare, not to suggest endorsement. If you make one, tag me @vlada.asana, I want to see it.
